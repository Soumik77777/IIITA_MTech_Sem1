Rationale: Trimmed k-means relies on heuristic search strategies like simulated annealing due to the lack of deterministic exhaustive algorithms. Static cooling schedules and rigid spherical radius updates fail to escape complex loss surfaces, leading to slow indefinite oscillations and premature convergence.
Testable prediction: An adaptive simulated annealing algorithm with dynamic cooling and variable cluster radii will achieve a lower final objective loss and require fewer iterations to stabilize than standard static cooling schedules across heavily contaminated high-dimensional datasets.
