**Evidence anchors used in the justifications**
- **[GGM99]** A central limit theorem for generalized trimmed k-means was established by García-Escudero, Gordaliza and Matrán, building on the consistency results of Cuesta-Albertos et al. (1997). Asymptotic-distribution gaps are therefore only partly open.
- **[A22]** Agrawal et al. give FPT approximation algorithms in k and m, with running time f(k,m)·n^O(1) and optimal approximation ratios for k-Median/k-Means with outliers.
- **[H23]** Huang et al. build ε-coresets of size O(m + poly(k/ε)) for robust clustering. They also show that a linear dependence on m cannot be avoided in coreset size. This makes "coreset removes m-dependence" hypotheses doubtful.
- **[IV26]** The pseudo-isolation framework uses a rank h ≥ 1 and evaluates thresholds with the Cantelli inequality. I could not retrieve the AF-BKM 2026 paper itself, so statements about what it already contains are uncertain.
- From prior knowledge: TCLUST and trimmed-determinant methods (García-Escudero et al. 2008; Gallegos–Ritter) cover non-spherical trimmed clustering. The "Trimming Tools" paper in your BibTeX already provides diagnostics for choosing k and α. Georgogiannis (2016) covers the 2/n breakdown and its restricted-separation remedy. MCD is a standard robust estimator. Concept-drift detectors (ADWIN, Page-Hinkley, EWMA) are standard.
